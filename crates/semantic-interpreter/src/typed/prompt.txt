Operations: project(field,alias); filter(predicate); order(field,direction=asc|desc,nulls=first|last); limit(count=unsigned integer). Row queries require a project. Aggregate queries require group, aggregate or metric outputs and prohibit ordinary project operations. At most one limit. Sorting is on source fields. Use nulls last when the request omits null ordering; this protocol defines that presentation default. Explicit null ordering overrides it.

Defaults cap each at 1,000,000, and the host may lower those caps. An offset without a complete requested ordering does not guarantee stable pages; do not invent ordering just to claim stability.

Aggregate queries use group(field,alias), aggregate(function=count|sum|min|max|avg,field,distinct,alias), metric(name,alias,applicability), and order_output(slot,direction,nulls). Grouping also projects its dimension. Project only the requested result columns; fields used solely for filters or ordering do not belong in the result. A request to sort by a field without specifying direction means ascending; do not request clarification merely for omitted direction. Explicit ascending/descending always takes precedence.
